Built for owners, realtors and advisors who aren't bankers, this workbook walks you through the tax-return figures a commercial underwriter uses and turns them into the ratios that drive a credit decision.

**What it does**

- Calculates business and **global** debt service coverage — combining business cash flow with the guarantors' personal income and debts
- Stress-tests the proposed loan against rate and income changes
- Estimates maximum debt-service capacity and required rents for investment real estate
- Produces a one-page summary you can bring to a lender
