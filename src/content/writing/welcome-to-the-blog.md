For more than twenty years I've sat on the lending side of the desk — as a credit analyst, a commercial lender, a CFO and, today, a business consultant. The same questions come up again and again: *How do I get approved? Where else can I find capital? What are my numbers actually telling me?*

This blog is where I'll answer them in plain language. Expect three kinds of posts:

- **Blog posts** — shorter notes on what I'm seeing in small-business lending, capital markets and the Columbus / Chattahoochee Valley business community.
- **Articles** — longer pieces on topics like underwriting, alternative capital and financial statements.
- **How-to guides** — step-by-step instruction you can use right away, for owners and for fellow instructors.

## Stay in touch

- Follow along with the [RSS feed](../../rss.xml), or connect with me on LinkedIn.
- Have a question you'd like answered in a future post? [Send it to me](../../contact) — questions from readers make the best topics.

Thanks for reading.

— Thomas
